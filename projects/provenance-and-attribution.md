Images are copied, cropped, recombined and regenerated as they move across the web. We trace these relationships back to their sources, reconstructing how a set of related images was derived from one another and attributing synthetic content to the identities and models behind it. Our work spans transformation-aware embeddings, large-scale provenance retrieval, and identity-based provenance for generated faces.
