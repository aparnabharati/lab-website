Generative models make it easy to copy an artist's style or edit a personal photo without consent. We study how to protect visual assets from unauthorized AI-based editing and mimicry, from imperceptible adversarial perturbations to watermarking, and we critically evaluate whether these protections actually hold up against the editing pipelines they are meant to disrupt.
