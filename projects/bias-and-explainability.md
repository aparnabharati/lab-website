Forensic detectors inherit biases from their training data and can rely on cues that have little to do with the manipulation itself. We examine how these systems perform across demographic groups, what they actually attend to, and how data diversity shapes their ability to generalise, with the aim of detectors whose decisions are fair and interpretable.
